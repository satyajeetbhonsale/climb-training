Daily traffic light · Pinky

Check each morning before climbing. The lower light wins — never argue yourself up.

Green

No symptoms. No morning stiffness. Pain-free to press on the right side of the pinky. Train as planned.

Yellow

Mild presence, no pain on climbing. Train, but no crimps smaller than 20 mm, no limit boulders, skip any fingerboard. This is the default for June.

Red

Pain on climb, sharp pain on press, swelling, or pain > 3/10. Stop the session. Ice 10 min. Rest 2–3 days. Restart at yellow. Two reds in two weeks → physio re-visit.

Stop and seek in-person assessment if: sharp pain on a specific move · swelling or visible deformity at any joint · audible pop or snap · pain at night that wakes you · loss of grip strength · numbness or tingling in any finger.
